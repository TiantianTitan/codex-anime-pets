# Burnice White reference selection

## Generation references

- `reference-01.webp` — official full-body outfit, hair, palette, body proportions, backpack, and weapon design. The weapons are identity evidence only and are omitted from the compact pet silhouette.
- `reference-02.jpeg` — front/back construction, jacket flame graphics, skirt, leg straps, boots, ponytails, and rear silhouette.
- `reference-03.webp` — chibi expression, face proportions, goggles, and energetic personality reference; visible text and watermarks are excluded from generation.

## Retained local originals

All eight supplied source files are preserved byte-for-byte under `references/source-originals/`. They are local-only reference material and must not enter the public character folder, README assets, QA publication directory, Git, or release ZIP.

## Production simplification

Burnice keeps her close-fitting fuel backpack silhouette, but the oversized handheld flamethrowers are not carried in the pet atlas. This preserves the recognizable outfit while keeping every action readable and safely padded inside a 192×208 cell.
