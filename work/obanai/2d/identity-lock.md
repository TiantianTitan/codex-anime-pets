# Obanai 2D identity lock

## Character invariants

- Humanoid chibi male with a compact full-body silhouette and calm, vigilant demeanor.
- Straight layered black shoulder-length hair with long angular front bangs and outward-tapered side/back locks.
- Heterochromia is mandatory in front view: the eye on the viewer's left is golden yellow; the eye on the viewer's right is teal-green. When the head turns, preserve the character-relative eye colors naturally rather than swapping them.
- White cloth bandages cover the mouth and lower face at all times. Do not expose a mouth, teeth, lips, or an unbandaged lower face.
- Black-and-white vertically striped haori with broad sleeves over a dark navy high-collared uniform, two visible gold buttons, and a white belt.
- White calf bandages and dark indigo sandals.
- Exactly one small white snake with subtle pale scale lines and red eyes remains physically wrapped around the neck and shoulders. It may follow head motion or shift its head slightly, but it must never detach, multiply, float, become a scarf, or turn green.

## Style lock

- Crisp polished 2D chibi sticker illustration.
- Bold clean dark outlines, flat readable color blocks, restrained cel shading, no painterly texture and no 3D/PVC material.
- Black, white, charcoal, and deep navy dominate; yellow and teal are limited to the eyes, red to the snake's tiny eyes.
- Compact proportions with an oversized head, short body and limbs, clean silhouette, and details large enough to survive 192×208 display.

## Exclusions

- No sword, parasol, tablet, boat, dock, furniture, scenery, floor, cast shadow, text, watermark, logo, or readable symbol.
- No additional snake, detached snake, floating effect, speed line, wave mark, dust, sparkle, punctuation, UI, paper, or prop introduced by an animation state.
- No alternate kimono-only outfit, exposed mouth, swapped eye colors, bright-green character pixels, or identity/style drift.
