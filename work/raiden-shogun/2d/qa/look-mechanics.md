# Raiden Shogun look mechanics

## Natural motion

Raiden Shogun looks around as a poised humanoid character. Her violet eyes lead each change, followed by a restrained head-and-neck turn and a very small upper-torso response. Her feet, lower body, scale, and baseline stay fixed. The long braid remains attached at the back of the head and follows the turn with a slight one-step lag; it never changes sides or swings independently. The floral hair ornament, kimono layers, obi, red cords, stockings, and sandals keep their canonical asymmetry and construction.

Her eyes remain the same anime eye design: iris, pupil, highlights, eyelids, and brows move together inside the original eye apertures. Do not add replacement eyes, slide detached pupils, stretch the face, rotate the whole sprite, or tilt the body as a flat cutout.

## Cardinal pose families

- **000 / up:** Broadly frontal. Both eyes remain readable, irises sit visibly high, upper lids open slightly, and the chin lifts. The neck and upper chest follow subtly while the lower body stays planted.
- **090 / screen-right:** The nose tip, pupils, and facial plane project unmistakably toward the image-right edge. The far eye narrows naturally, the near cheek contour leads, and the head/neck yaw is supported by a restrained shoulder turn. Hair and braid trail continuously behind the turn.
- **180 / down:** Broadly frontal. The chin tucks, irises sit visibly low, upper lids lower slightly, and the bangs overlap more of the eye area. The upper torso bows only a little; feet and hem baseline remain fixed.
- **270 / screen-left:** The nose tip, pupils, and facial plane project unmistakably toward the image-left edge, visibly opposing 090. The far eye narrows naturally, the near cheek contour leads, and hair plus braid follow without flipping identity sides.

## Motion budget and continuity

Each 22.5-degree step changes eye position, eyelids, head yaw/pitch, neck, shoulder angle, and braid follow-through by a comparable small amount. The figure keeps one shared height, foot line, lower-body anchor, and head volume. `157.5 → 180`, `180 → 202.5`, and `337.5 → 000` must feel like ordinary single steps, with no sudden frontal/profile snap, scale pop, re-centering jump, ornament flip, or braid teleport.

There is no held prop in the canonical pet. Do not introduce a sword, lightning effect, halo, aura, floating Electro symbol, shadow, or scenery in any look pose.
