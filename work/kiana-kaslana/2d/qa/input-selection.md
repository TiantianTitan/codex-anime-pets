# Grounding input selection

The source folder retains all seven user references byte-for-byte. Generation jobs use at most five attached images because the built-in image generator accepts no more than five references per call.

- Base: all five prepared combat-suit, face, and alternate-angle references.
- Standard rows: the strongest official full-body combat-suit reference, the clearest twin-pistol full-body reference, the strongest alternate-angle reference, the row layout guide, and the approved canonical base.
- Cardinals: two complementary identity references, the cardinal layout guide, canonical base, and approved standard-row contact sheet.
- Look row 9: one official identity reference, layout guide, canonical base, approved contact sheet, and approved cardinal strip.
- Look row 10: layout guide, canonical base, approved contact sheet, approved cardinal strip, and the completed row 9 for loop continuity.

The school-uniform and casual references remain archived locally but are excluded from generation so their clothing cannot leak into the canonical white/black/orange Valkyrie design.
