- The eyes and eyelids initiate each look, followed by a small head turn or tilt and a restrained upper-torso follow-through.
- The long indigo hair follows the head with a slight lag; keep its total mass, paired side lengths, and sculpted silhouette stable.
- Preserve the asymmetric white lightning-shaped hair mark on the same character-side front lock in every direction.

- Direction anchors are semantic: `000` looks up, `090` looks screen-right, `180` looks down, and `270` looks screen-left.
- Intermediate directions must interpolate the gaze, eyelids, head, hair, and soft 3D lighting continuously. Avoid sudden size, baseline, costume, material, or silhouette jumps.
- Maintain the approved polished chibi game/PVC rendering and the same full-body scale as the standard rows.
