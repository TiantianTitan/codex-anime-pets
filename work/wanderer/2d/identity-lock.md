# Wanderer 2D identity lock

## Immutable identity

- Compact full-body anime chibi with a large head, short slim body, pale skin, and a cool, self-possessed expression.
- Short indigo-blue bob with straight fringe, longer tapered side/back locks, and violet-blue eyes.
- Wide, flat circular kasa hat: deep indigo top, bright cyan underside, antique-gold structural rim and ornaments, plus two long cyan patterned ribbons hanging from the hat.
- Layered outfit: black high-neck underlayer, white short outer robe, cyan waist wrap, asymmetric deep-blue shoulder mantle, black pleated shorts, white leg wraps, and dark open sandals.
- Gold circular chest ornament with a turquoise center, purple waist cords and tassels, and blue/cyan fabric tails are required identity anchors.
- Youthful masculine presentation; faintly mischievous confidence rather than a cheerful or timid expression.

## True-side and asymmetry rules

- The deep-blue shoulder mantle and circular chest ornament arrangement are asymmetric and must remain on their canonical sides rather than being mirrored blindly.
- Hat ornaments, hanging ribbons, waist cords, fabric tails, and shoulder layers must stay physically attached and preserve their attachment points as the body turns.
- The two travel directions must be generated separately. Do not derive `running-left` by mirroring `running-right`.
- Side and rear views should use the simplest conservative continuation of visible costume structure; do not invent new emblems, weapons, or back accessories.

## Style lock

- Polished 2D anime chibi sticker, crisp dark outline, clean cel shading, simplified readable costume blocks, and clear facial features at 192×208.
- The hat must remain broad enough to identify the character but compact enough to keep generous margins in every frame.
- Hat ribbons may sway with delayed follow-through, but must stay shortened, attached, and inside their own frame slot.
- No 3D rendering, painterly texture, photographic treatment, fine lace noise, or overly thin lines.

## Animation allowances

- Expressions, blinking, restrained head/torso movement, hand gestures, hat-ribbon follow-through, and fabric sway may change with the state.
- Idle uses subtle breathing and one brief synchronized blink; first and last frames keep both eyes open.
- Waving uses the hand only; jumping uses body height only; failed uses posture and expression; working and review use focused body language without new props.

## Prohibited additions

- No weapon, cats, readable logo, text, UI, scenery, ground, floor shadow, glow, detached Anemo symbols, floating leaves, sparkles, speed lines, dust, or motion arcs.
- No detached hat ornaments or ribbons, no cropped brim, no neighboring-pose fragments, and no visible layout guides.
