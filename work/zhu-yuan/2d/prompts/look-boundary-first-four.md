## Hard gates

- The face must progress monotonically from frontal/down to stronger image-left/down-left. No slot may point image-right.
- The ponytail must progress monotonically `image-left/rear → left-center/behind → centered/occluded → image-right/rear`. Do not jump sides between adjacent poses.
- Preserve the red tie and orange streak as the same physical features following the head/ponytail; do not mirror the character design.
- Keep body, feet, pelvis, and lower torso stable; eyes/head/neck lead the look motion.

All directions are literal viewer/screen coordinates. IMAGE-LEFT is the left edge of the output canvas.
