# 🐾 Codex Pet

### What if your coding assistant had a pet?

**Codex Pet** is a tiny digital companion that lives alongside your coding workflow.

While you work with Codex, your pet watches, reacts, sleeps, celebrates, gets bored, and occasionally judges your code.

Because coding with AI doesn't have to feel lonely.

---

## ✨ Meet your coding companion

Your pet reacts to what happens in your workflow.

```text
🧠 Codex is thinking...
🐱 Mochi is watching carefully.

██████████░░░░  thinking...
```

When your task succeeds:

```text
✓ Task completed!

🐱 Mochi is extremely happy.
✨ +10 happiness
🍪 Wants a snack.
```

When something breaks:

```text
✗ Tests failed

🐱 Mochi has no idea what happened.

(╥﹏╥)
```

Leave your terminal for too long?

```text
🐱 Mochi is sleeping...

zZz...
```

---

# 🎮 Features

* 🐾 **Reactive pets** — Your pet responds to what Codex is doing
* 😴 **Idle & sleep states** — Pets get tired when nothing happens
* 🎉 **Task celebrations** — Successful tasks make your pet happy
* 😵 **Error reactions** — Failed commands might confuse your pet
* 🍪 **Pet interactions** — Feed, play, or check in with your pet
* 🎨 **Custom skins** — Create your own pets
* 🧩 **Plugin system** — Add behaviors, reactions, and integrations
* 💾 **Persistent state** — Your pet remembers its mood
* 🌍 **Community pets** — Share your creations with everyone

# 🐱 Every developer deserves a coding pet


| ------------------- | --------------- |

| Build failed        | 😨 Confused     |
| Long coding session | 🔥 Motivated    |
